The whole collection

A living inventory of core games, expansions, Gearloc packs, promos and accessories that affect solo play. Nothing is considered covered until its scope and rules are checked.

You have the complete collection, so the target is full coverage—not a base-game-only guide. The initial list below is a research framework, not a claim that every product category or edition has been exhaustively verified.

For every product we will record

  • Official product name and edition or printing where relevant.
  • Whether it is standalone, an expansion, an add-on or an accessory.
  • Components, Gearlocs, Tyrants, Baddies and encounter content included.
  • Whether it changes setup or rules, and what other content it requires.
  • Official rulebook, FAQ and errata references.
  • Solo-specific opportunities, restrictions and recommendations.

Completeness rule

The research register will distinguish “official product identified,” “rules reviewed,” “solo implications written” and “strategy tested.” This avoids marking an expansion complete merely because its name appears on a list.
