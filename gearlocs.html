CHARACTER LAB · PATH 03

Gearloc academy

Every Gearloc gets an individual dossier: what it does, how it develops, what it struggles with and how to pilot it in solo play.

The full roster will be established from your collection and official product materials. This prevents missing a promo Gearloc or accidentally treating an expansion-exclusive rule as universal.

What every dossier will contain

At a glance

Role, learning complexity, core mechanic, strengths, weaknesses and recommended first build.

Dice & skills

Starting options, skill-tree choices, priority dice, development sequence and key synergies.

Solo plan

Party-size considerations, survivability, action economy, encounter approach and Tyrant preparation.

Build testing

Opening plan, milestone decisions, common traps, matchup notes and confidence level of recommendations.

How recommendations will be labelled

  • Rules-confirmed: supported by official rule text or publisher clarification.
  • Strategy recommendation: a reasoned approach, not a mandatory rule.
  • Tested: observed across recorded play sessions.
  • Hypothesis: promising idea that still needs practical validation.
