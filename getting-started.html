LEARN TO PLAY · PATH 01

Your first solo run

A staged route through choosing a Tyrant, preparing the game, resolving Encounters, fighting battles and developing your Gearlocs. This guide deliberately avoids unverified card counts or setup shortcuts.

Start with the publisher's current material. Open the official Too Many Bones support hub. It links the base-game, Undertow, Unbreakable and Splice & Dice rulebooks, Gearloc reference sheets, the Baddie Skills reference and the official FAQ. Check the rulebook for the exact edition and content you are setting up.
Correction — do not use the earlier card recipe. An earlier answer in this project suggested a fixed pile of 8 General Encounter cards plus Tyrant Encounter cards, then selecting Special Encounter cards from numbered groups 001–004 and 005–008. That recipe, and the related assumption about the run length, were not verified against the current official rulebook. They are withdrawn. Do not prepare your deck using those instructions. The exact Drellen setup and card sequence remain under official-source verification.

Stage 1 — Lock the game scope

  1. Choose which game is the adventure's rules base: the original Too Many Bones, Undertow or Unbreakable. Do not mix setup instructions from different rulebooks.
  2. Choose the Tyrant and read its official card and any Tyrant-specific setup instructions before sorting Encounter cards.
  3. Choose the Gearloc party using the setup rules for that game and the number of Gearlocs you intend to control.
  4. Decide which expansions or add-ons are actually being used. Leave unrelated content out until the core setup is clear.

Checkpoint: count players and Gearlocs separately

For a solo multi-handed game, write down both figures: human players and Gearlocs in the party. CTG's official FAQ defines party size by Gearlocs only (including KO'd Gearlocs); Lab Rats count as one Gearloc, while companions and temporary allies do not. Party size normally matches player count except when someone controls multiple Gearlocs.

Do not replace a party-size value with the number of humans at the table. Follow the relevant rulebook or card for any setup scaling.

Official CTG clarification ↗

Playing multi-handed? Use the multi-handed table procedure for a practical two-Gearloc setup and battle-management aid. It keeps strategy separate from official rules and does not invent a card recipe.

Stage 2 — Gather components from the rulebook checklist

  • Each chosen Gearloc's mat, chip, dice and official reference sheet.
  • The correct Battle Mat, initiative components and shared dice for the selected game.
  • The Encounter cards, Tyrant card(s), Baddie chips, Loot and other components specifically named by the rulebook and Tyrant instructions.
  • Any expansion-specific components only if that content is included in this adventure.
  • The current official rulebook and the official Too Many Bones FAQ index within reach.

Do not infer a component count from another edition or from the total number of cards printed on a product page. Use the setup section of the actual rulebook.

Stage 3 — Prepare the Encounter sequence

Follow the selected game's setup section and the chosen Tyrant's instructions exactly. Keep General, Special and Tyrant Encounter cards in the groups and order specified by those sources. Do not add, remove, shuffle or select numbered cards based on a tutorial, an old session, or an assistant-generated recipe unless the official rulebook or card tells you to.

Setup verification checkpoint

Before the first day, be able to point to the official source for each of these: required day count, number and type of Encounter cards, any Special Encounter selection, placement of Tyrant Encounters, and any extra cards or components. If one item has no official citation yet, pause that part of setup rather than guessing.

Stage 4 — Resolve each Encounter carefully

  1. Read the whole Encounter card before choosing an option.
  2. Identify its stated success, failure, reward and any special battle conditions.
  3. Set up the battle exactly as the card and rulebook direct.
  4. Resolve the Encounter's outcome and rewards as written; then update the campaign and Gearloc state.
  5. Use the rulebook's recovery and progression instructions before moving to the next day.

If you draw Gearlocs Need Food Badly

Pause before using the ordinary battle flow. CTG's clarification gives this Encounter its own critter movement, alternating critter/Gearloc activations and restrictions on Skills and Backup Plans. Follow the worked official procedure in the Rules reference.

Stage 5 — Run battles without skipping timing

  1. Read the battle's special rules and victory/failure condition before placing units.
  2. Set up Gearlocs, Baddies, initiative and any required tokens or dice using the official instructions.
  3. Resolve turns and rounds in the order specified by the rulebook. Track movement, valid targets, attacks, Defence, effects and status changes.
  4. Resolve start-of-turn effects in the player-chosen order; remember that Fatigue happens at the beginning of a Round, not a turn. See the official FAQ battle reference.
  5. Stop immediately when the official battle-end condition is met. Do not continue taking turns after the battle has ended.

Stage 6 — Develop the Gearlocs

Use the rulebook and each Gearloc's reference sheet for available development choices, dice, skills and recovery. The academy's future build recommendations will be labelled as strategy, not rules, and will state the party size and Tyrant assumptions they are based on.

Stage 7 — Review the run

  • Record the game/expansion, Tyrant, party size and Gearlocs used.
  • Note any rules question and the exact official FAQ or rulebook section that resolved it.
  • Record the decisions that worked, the main source of damage and any unused dice or skills.
  • Keep an unresolved question unresolved until an official CTG source answers it.
