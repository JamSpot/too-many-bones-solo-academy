LEARN TO PLAY · PATH 01

Your first solo run

A calm, staged route through setup, encounters, development and the final challenge. Use your edition's rulebook alongside this guide; this page is a learning scaffold, not a substitute for the full rules.

Before you begin: choose a Tyrant and party size using the setup rules for your game and expansions. The exact setup, day count, encounter deck and available components can vary by mode and content.

Stage 1 — Set up your table

  • Choose the Tyrant and confirm the required number of days and encounter setup.
  • Choose the Gearloc or Gearlocs you will control.
  • Gather the correct Gearloc mats, dice, chips, Baddie chips, encounter cards, loot and reference materials.
  • Prepare the battle mat and initiative area, and keep the rulebook within reach.
  • Check any expansion-specific setup instructions before starting.

Stage 2 — Understand the day loop

Each day presents a limited opportunity to face encounters, improve your Gearloc and prepare for the Tyrant. Read the encounter completely before committing to a choice, and resolve its instructions in order. Some choices provide immediate benefits; others may create future costs or risks.

Before the encounter

Check your current health, available skills and dice, party condition, remaining time and any special rules.

After the encounter

Resolve all rewards and consequences, update your character and campaign state, then confirm the next step from the rules.

Stage 3 — Run a battle deliberately

  1. Read the encounter or Tyrant instructions and build the Baddie lineup exactly as directed.
  2. Set up initiative and apply any effects that happen before the first turn.
  3. Resolve turns in the correct order, tracking movement, targeting, attack, defence and special abilities.
  4. Keep health, statuses, defeated Baddies and other battle effects visible.
  5. At the end of each round, resolve end-of-round effects before beginning the next round.
  6. When the battle ends, follow the rules for victory, defeat, rewards and campaign progression.
Rules check: exact timing, targeting, damage mitigation and status interactions matter. When an edge case comes up, consult the current official rulebook and FAQ rather than relying on a simplified summary.

Stage 4 — Improve with intention

Do not treat every die as automatically useful. Ask what your build needs next: reliable damage, survivability, mobility, control, support or consistency. A strong choice usually improves the plan you are actually using rather than adding an impressive ability you cannot afford to develop.

Stage 5 — Prepare for the Tyrant

  • Read the Tyrant's special rules before the final battle.
  • Check whether your build can answer the Tyrant's key threats.
  • Enter the final challenge with your available resources and campaign state correctly recorded.
  • After the game, note which decisions helped, which dice were underused and what you would change.

First-session debrief

  • Which rules did I need to look up repeatedly?
  • What caused the most damage or lost tempo?
  • Which dice or skills did I rarely use?
  • Was my risk-taking appropriate for the remaining days?
  • What one change will I test next run?
