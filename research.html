EVIDENCE & PROGRESS

Research register

A transparent record of what has been checked against Chip Theory Games' own materials, what has been written, and what still needs verification or table testing.

Rules-source policy: for rules, setup, card order, Gearloc abilities, Tyrant mechanics and encounter procedures, the only authority used here is Chip Theory Games' official rulebooks, reference sheets, FAQs, errata and clarifications. Official product pages may confirm product contents or player count, but are not used to override rulebooks. No fan guide, forum, video commentary or third-party rules summary is treated as rules authority.

Official sources rechecked on 22 November 2026

  • Too Many Bones — official support hub — publisher-provided links to the base-game, Undertow, Unbreakable and Splice & Dice rulebooks, Gearloc reference sheets, Baddie Skills reference and FAQ. CTG states that its FAQ is kept updated with rulings, clarifications and errata.
  • Too Many Bones — official FAQ index — categories include Battle, Baddie Skills and Encounter Terms, Tyrants, specific Encounters, Loot and Gearloc-specific questions.
  • Too Many Bones — official product page — confirms the base game is listed for 1–4 players and identifies the original four Gearlocs as Picket, Patches, Boomer and Tantrum. Used only for product-scope checks.
  • What units does party size include? — official clarification modified 14 July 2023; rechecked 19 November 2026. Confirms that party size is Gearloc count, not necessarily human-player count in multi-handed play.
  • “Gearlocs Need Food Badly” Encounter clarification — modified 15 September 2026; official procedure for critter setup and movement, alternating activations, permitted Gearloc actions and Buff HP reward.
  • Can units besides Gearlocs have Buff HP? — modified 5 July 2024; only Gearlocs can have Buff HP because it is held in their Prep Area.
  • Baddie Skills and Encounter Terms — official FAQ category — includes Poison, Flee, Signal, Thick Skin, Engulf/Break and other Baddie keyword clarifications. The individual articles are linked in the Rules reference.
  • Tyrants — official FAQ category — currently lists six Tyrant rulings, including Rol's movement clarification; rechecked 22 November 2026.

Verified rulings added to the academy

QuestionVerified official clarificationPublisher source
Start-of-turn timingResolve start-of-turn effects in the player's chosen order. Fatigue occurs at the beginning of a Round and affects all Baddies and Gearlocs simultaneously.CTG FAQ ↗
Baddie Active DefenceBaddies do not reroll Active Defence; subtract Active Defence dice already on them from their Defence stat when rolling.CTG FAQ ↗
Movement blockingGearlocs and Baddies cannot move through other units on the Battle Mat.CTG FAQ ↗
Valid attack targetsAttacking requires a valid target unless a card or Skill says otherwise.CTG FAQ ↗
Battle endBattle ends immediately when its stated success/failure condition is met; a Tyrant battle ends when the Tyrant is defeated even if Baddies remain.CTG FAQ ↗
Simultaneous Fatigue defeatIf Fatigue removes the last HP from the remaining Gearlocs and Baddies at the same time, the battle is a failure.CTG FAQ ↗
Effect dice on removed unitsRemove Effect dice when their unit is removed from the Battle Mat.CTG FAQ ↗
Patches healing rangePatches does not need to be adjacent to a Gearloc he heals.CTG FAQ ↗
Party size and multi-handed playParty size counts Gearlocs only (including KO'd Gearlocs); Lab Rats count as one; companions and temporary allies do not. Party size normally matches player count except when a player controls multiple Gearlocs.CTG FAQ ↗
Buff HP ownershipOnly Gearlocs can have Buff HP; non-Gearloc grants are ignored.CTG FAQ ↗
Poison stackingPoison is not cumulative; the more recently applied Poison value replaces the previous value, even when lower.CTG FAQ ↗
FleeA fleeing Baddie is removed to the defeated stack; the closest opposing unit with Loot discards one. Flee plus Treasure does not drop a Loot die unless the Baddie is defeated normally.CTG FAQ ↗
SignalSignal's number is the number of Rounds in which one Baddie is added to the Battle Queue on the Signal Baddie's turn—not the number added at once.CTG FAQ ↗
Thick SkinRemove Defence dice first, then reduce total incoming damage by the printed value (or 1 if none is shown); True Damage effects can help mitigate it.CTG FAQ ↗
Engulf and BreakWhen Engulf damages a Baddie with Break, the Engulf Baddie's Attack dice are exhausted.CTG FAQ ↗
Gearlocs Need Food BadlyOfficial special-Encounter setup, critter movement, alternating activations and reward procedure documented.CTG FAQ ↗
Tyrant-specific movement (Rol)Rol identifies the strongest opposing unit by HP and moves to a reachable Rock space adjacent to it; this movement can exceed two spaces. She considers Rok an opponent but not the other Baddies.CTG FAQ ↗

Corrections and unresolved items

  • Withdrawn: the earlier unverified Drellen deck recipe (8 General Encounter cards plus Tyrant cards, then Special Encounter selections from 001–004 and 005–008). It must not be used as a setup instruction.
  • Unresolved: the exact current official setup and numbered Encounter-card sequence for Drellen. No exact sequence will be published until the base-game rulebook and Tyrant-specific instructions have both been checked.
  • Not yet tested: any Gearloc build or solo strategy recommendation. A rules clarification is not evidence that a particular build is optimal.

Evidence and editorial standards

  • Link each rules claim to the official source that supports it.
  • Record game edition, expansion, party size, Gearlocs and Tyrant wherever they affect a conclusion.
  • Label content as official rule, strategy recommendation, table-tested or unresolved.
  • Never publish an exact card sequence, encounter count or setup recipe based on inference.
  • Do not reproduce complete rulebooks or card text; summarise and link to the publisher.
  • Test navigation and deployment after site changes, and report failures rather than claiming success.
