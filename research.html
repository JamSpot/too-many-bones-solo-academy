Source hierarchy

  1. Official rulebooks and reference sheets: primary source for rules and component instructions.
  2. Official FAQ and errata: use for clarifications and corrections.
  3. Official product pages: confirm product scope, included components and compatibility.
  4. Experienced-player strategy: useful for tactics, but separate from authoritative rules.
  5. Recorded solo play: supports local observations and testing, with assumptions documented.

Official starting points

  • Too Many Bones — official support hub — rulebooks, FAQs, reference sheets and errata where provided.
  • Chip Theory Games — publisher product information and official updates.

Research record template

Question: the rule, matchup or strategy being investigated.

Scope: game edition, expansion, party size, Gearlocs and Tyrant.

Primary evidence: source title, section/page and date checked.

Conclusion: confirmed rule, strategic recommendation or unresolved hypothesis.

Follow-up: related questions, test run or required correction.

Editorial standards

  • Do not reproduce entire rulebooks or card text; summarise and point to the official source.
  • Flag edition-specific rules and interactions that need verification.
  • Do not claim a build is tested without recording the test conditions.
  • Fix broken navigation and broken build checks before calling a release complete.
  • Keep this register updated as new pages are researched.
