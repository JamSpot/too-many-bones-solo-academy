Rules reference

A practical quick reference built from Chip Theory Games' own rulebooks, reference sheets, FAQs and errata. Every rule clarification below links to the publisher's support article.

Authority rule: Chip Theory Games is the only rules authority used by this academy. This page summarises selected official FAQ clarifications; it is not a complete rulebook. Always check the rulebook for your game/expansion and the relevant card or Gearloc reference sheet first. The publisher says its Too Many Bones FAQ is kept updated with rulings, clarifications and errata. Open the official support hub ↗.

Battle rulings verified against official FAQs

Checked 15 November 2026. These are publisher clarifications, not house rules.

1. Start-of-turn effects

Resolve start-of-turn effects in the order chosen by the player. The official example allows Patches to heal before Poison damage is applied. Fatigue is different: it occurs at the beginning of a Round, not a turn, and affects all Baddies and Gearlocs simultaneously.

Read CTG's start-of-turn clarification ↗

2. Active Defence on Baddies

Baddies do not reroll their Active Defence dice on their turn. When rolling Defence, subtract the number of Active Defence dice already on the Baddie from its Defence stat. CTG's example: a Baddie with Defence 2 and one Active Defence die rolls one Defence die.

Read CTG's Active Defence clarification ↗

3. Movement through units

Gearlocs and Baddies cannot move through other units on the Battle Mat. Other units restrict movement, so positioning can be used to block routes and protect a Gearloc from a melee attacker.

Read CTG's movement clarification ↗

4. Attacking without a valid target

An attack requires a valid target unless a card or Skill specifically says otherwise. Do not roll Attack dice solely to try to generate Bones when no valid target exists.

Read CTG's valid-target clarification ↗

5. When a battle ends

A battle ends immediately when the last Baddie is defeated, or when the last Gearloc is KO'd. If an Encounter states a different success or failure condition, the battle ends as soon as that condition is met. In a Tyrant battle, defeating the Tyrant ends the battle even if Baddies remain.

Read CTG's battle-end clarification ↗

6. Fatigue defeats both sides at once

If Fatigue removes the last HP from the remaining Gearlocs and Baddies simultaneously, the battle is a failure. A Gearloc must remain after all Baddies are defeated for the battle to count as a success.

Read CTG's simultaneous-Fatigue clarification ↗

7. Effect dice when a unit leaves the mat

Effect dice can only remain on units that are on the Battle Mat. When a unit is removed from the mat, remove any Effect dice on it.

Read CTG's Effect-dice clarification ↗

8. Patches' healing range

Patches does not need to be adjacent to another Gearloc to heal them. This is a Patches-specific clarification; it does not change other targeting or range rules.

Read CTG's Patches clarification ↗

How to verify a rules question

  1. Identify the exact game, expansion, Gearloc, Tyrant, card and timing window involved.
  2. Check the relevant official rulebook and reference sheet from the CTG Too Many Bones support hub.
  3. Search the official Too Many Bones FAQ index for a clarification or erratum.
  4. Record the conclusion with the exact official source link and the date checked.
  5. If no official source resolves the question, mark it unresolved and ask CTG support. Do not fill the gap with a fan interpretation.
