Rules verification workflow

  1. Identify the exact question and the components involved.
  2. Check the current official rulebook and any relevant expansion rulebook.
  3. Check publisher FAQ and errata for clarifications or changed wording.
  4. Record the source, page or section and a concise explanation in the research notes.
  5. Keep a separate example if the interaction is difficult to remember.

Quick table checklist

  • All Baddie and Gearloc information is visible.
  • Initiative order is unambiguous.
  • Health, statuses and temporary effects are tracked.
  • Range and targeting are checked before resolving attacks.
  • Round-end timing is resolved before the next round begins.
  • Encounter rewards and campaign changes are not forgotten.
