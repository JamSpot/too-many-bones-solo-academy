TACTICAL THINKING · PATH 06

Solo strategy lab

Understand the trade-offs behind a decision, not just a list of supposedly optimal moves. Strategy pages will be grounded in rules, matchups and recorded tests.

The core solo questions

True solo or multiple Gearlocs?

Compare cognitive load, action economy, scaling and how much character synergy you gain or lose. Exact setup and difficulty assumptions must be recorded.

What should I develop?

Balance stats, skill access, reliable dice, survival and the specific threat profile of your chosen Tyrant.

When should I take a risk?

Consider current health, remaining days, reward value, failure costs and whether a later encounter becomes harder.

How do I use the battle mat?

Prioritise positioning, initiative, range, target access and preventing dangerous enemies from taking effective turns.

Decision framework

  1. State the goal: what outcome must this turn or encounter achieve?
  2. Identify constraints: action options, available dice, position, health and special rules.
  3. Compare choices: expected benefit, failure risk and future cost.
  4. Check interactions: initiative, statuses, targeting and ability timing.
  5. Choose and review: after resolution, note whether the assumptions held.
