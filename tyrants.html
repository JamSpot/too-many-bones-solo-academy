Tyrants & Baddies

Prepare for the enemy before the final battle. Build Tyrant dossiers and learn how Baddie abilities shape positioning, priorities and risk.

Tyrant dossier template

Know the fight

  • Setup and special battle rules
  • Victory condition and timing
  • Unique mechanics and phases
  • Relevant FAQ or errata

Build a plan

  • Priority threats and targets
  • Damage and control requirements
  • Survivability and recovery needs
  • Gearloc synergies and counters

Baddie analysis framework

For each enemy family and relevant individual Baddie, the guide will record its role, movement, range, attack profile, keywords, special ability and the tactical problem it creates. The aim is to explain what the enemy changes about your turn—not simply restate its stats.

Battle planning checklist

  • Read all special rules before placing the first Baddie.
  • Identify enemies that can create immediate defeat risks.
  • Check initiative and likely order of actions.
  • Estimate whether a target can be defeated before it acts.
  • Account for movement, range, obstacles and target restrictions.
  • Track ongoing effects and trigger timing precisely.
  • Reassess priorities whenever a new Baddie enters or the board state changes.
Important: matchup advice depends on Gearloc, party size, difficulty and the particular edition or expansion rules. Dossiers will state their assumptions instead of presenting one universal answer.

Research order

  1. Catalogue all Tyrants from the owned collection.
  2. Transcribe only the minimum necessary reference data and link back to official sources.
  3. Record special mechanics and any FAQ clarifications.
  4. Build tactical plans for different Gearloc and party-size contexts.
  5. Separate verified mechanics from matchup opinions and playtest results.
